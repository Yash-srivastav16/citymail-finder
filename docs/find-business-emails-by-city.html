How to find business emails by city

When your research covers multiple locations, a city-by-city process can help keep searches and findings organized. CityMail Finder lets you prepare a list of cities, review publicly visible email addresses in Google result text and export unique findings.

STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE

Build a repeatable city research process

Start with a clear list and use the same search approach consistently. The quality of the results depends on the search query, the available public information and the locations you choose.

  1. 1

    Create your location list

    Prepare a spreadsheet with a column named City. You can also enter cities manually in the extension.

  2. 2

    Define the search phrase

    Use a relevant phrase for the type of business or public contact information you are researching. Keep the city placeholder in the query where required.

  3. 3

    Choose the number of pages

    Set how many Google result pages to review for each city. More pages can take longer and may increase the chance of provider rate limits.

  4. 4

    Review and export

    Check the unique email addresses collected in your browser and export the list to Excel.

Tips for better location-based research

A

Keep city names consistent

Use clear city names and avoid mixing unrelated location labels in the same list.

B

Use a focused query

Choose a search phrase that matches the business category and contact information you need to review.

C

Check before outreach

Search visibility does not prove an address is valid or that the owner has consented to receive marketing messages.

i

What this workflow does not do

CityMail Finder extracts only email addresses visible in Google search result text. It does not crawl company websites, verify deliverability, bypass CAPTCHA or override search provider limits. If a verification prompt appears, complete it manually. Follow the search provider's terms and applicable privacy and marketing laws.

FAQ

Finding business emails by city

Can I add several cities at once?

Yes. Import a CSV or Excel file with a City column, or add multiple cities manually.

Does it search every business in a city?

No. Search engines do not guarantee complete coverage. The extension reviews the configured number of result pages for the query you choose.

Are the collected emails verified?

No. The extension identifies email-shaped text visible in search results; it does not test whether an address exists or can receive messages.

Where are results stored?

Collected results are stored locally in your browser. See the Privacy Policy for details.
