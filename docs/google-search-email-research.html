SEARCH RESULT RESEARCH

Review email addresses visible in Google Search results.

CityMail Finder is a browser extension for organizing email addresses that appear in Google search result text. Set a query, provide cities, review result pages and export unique findings — without crawling the linked websites.

HOW IT WORKS

Search result text, not website crawling

It is important to understand the source of the collected information. The extension reviews the text rendered on Google result pages for your configured query and city.

01

Search pages

The extension opens Google search result pages using your query and city input.

02

Visible text only

Email addresses are extracted from text visible on the result page. Linked business sites are not visited for extraction.

03

Local organization

Unique results are saved in browser storage and can be exported to Excel.

VERIFICATION AND LIMITS

Keep provider controls in place

  1. 1

    Expect provider limits

    Automated queries can trigger rate limits, unusual-traffic notices or CAPTCHA challenges.

  2. 2

    Complete challenges manually

    If Google asks for verification, complete it yourself in the browser. The extension does not solve or bypass challenges.

  3. 3

    Resume only when available

    Continue the workflow after the search page is accessible again. Do not attempt to circumvent access restrictions.

  4. 4

    Use data responsibly

    Review each result and follow applicable privacy, data protection and marketing rules before contacting anyone.

i

Not an email verification or sending service

CityMail Finder does not verify deliverability, send messages, guarantee that an address belongs to a particular business or provide permission to contact an address. Search results can be incomplete, outdated or inaccurate. Follow Google's terms and applicable laws. Do not use collected addresses for unsolicited bulk email.

Read the Privacy Policy to understand local data handling.

FAQ

Google search email research FAQ

Does CityMail Finder scrape complete websites?

No. It extracts email addresses visible in Google search result text only. It does not crawl linked websites.

Does it bypass Google CAPTCHA?

No. CAPTCHA and other verification challenges must be completed manually by the user. The extension does not bypass provider restrictions.

Are the results always accurate?

No. Search results can be incomplete or outdated, and an email-shaped string is not proof that an address is active or associated with the intended business.

Can I use the results for bulk marketing?

You are responsible for complying with applicable privacy, data protection, anti-spam and marketing laws. Do not send unsolicited bulk email.
