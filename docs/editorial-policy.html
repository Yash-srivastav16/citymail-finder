Editorial policy

Our guides are intended to help readers understand business contact research, lead generation workflows and the limitations of related tools.

Research and writing

We write for practical use, explain terms in plain language and distinguish product capabilities from general advice. We do not claim that CityMail Finder verifies addresses or guarantees lead quality.

Product accuracy

Product descriptions are checked against the current extension workflow. If functionality changes, relevant pages should be reviewed before or alongside the release.

Sources and updates

Where a guide discusses third-party policies, readers are directed to official sources where practical. Third-party terms, pricing and features may change; readers should confirm current details with the provider.

Corrections

If you find an error, outdated statement or unclear explanation, please report it through GitHub Issues. We will review valid corrections and update affected pages.

Commercial relationships

Any future sponsored or affiliate relationship will be disclosed clearly on the relevant page. We do not currently claim endorsements or partnerships that have not been established.
