HOW IT WORKS

From city list to an organized email file

  1. 1

    Add your cities

    Type locations or import a CSV/XLSX spreadsheet with a City column.

  2. 2

    Review Google result pages

    CityMail Finder opens search pages and reads only the visible text on those result pages. It does not crawl the linked business websites.

  3. 3

    Handle verification yourself

    If a Google verification challenge appears, the search pauses. Complete the challenge manually in the browser, then resume when the page is available again.

  4. 4

    Export your results

    Review the unique addresses saved in your browser and export them to an Excel file.

i

Use search responsibly

Google may restrict automated queries, show verification challenges or change its result pages. Manual CAPTCHA completion does not grant permission to automate searches or override provider limits. Use the tool only in ways permitted by the search provider's terms and applicable privacy and marketing laws. Do not use collected addresses for unsolicited bulk email.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does CityMail Finder search?

It runs your configured query on Google Search using the cities you provide and extracts email addresses visible in the search result page text.

Does it visit or crawl business websites?

No. The current extension extracts email addresses visible on Google search result pages. It does not crawl the linked websites.

What happens when Google shows a CAPTCHA?

The workflow pauses and leaves the Google tab available so you can complete the verification yourself. CityMail Finder does not solve, hide or bypass the challenge.

Where are my results stored?

The extension saves collected result records in your browser's IndexedDB storage. The current extension workflow does not upload the collected email list to a CityMail Finder account or server.

Can I export results?

Yes. The extension exports unique email addresses to an Excel workbook.

Is CityMail Finder free?

CityMail Finder is being introduced as a free initial release. Any future pricing or paid features will be clearly announced before they are introduced.
