ABOUT THE PROJECT

About CityMail Finder

CityMail Finder is a focused Chrome extension project designed to make one part of business contact research easier to organize: reviewing email-shaped text visible in search results and exporting unique findings.

What the product does

The current extension lets users add cities manually or import a spreadsheet, configure a search phrase, review Google Search result pages, deduplicate visible email-shaped strings, save results locally in the browser and export them to Excel.

What it does not do

It does not crawl linked company websites, verify mailbox existence or deliverability, confirm ownership or consent, enrich contact profiles, or send email. Results can be incomplete or inaccurate and require independent review.

Our approach

We aim to describe capabilities accurately, explain limitations, protect user privacy and publish practical educational material. Read our editorial policy and privacy policy.
