WHY USE A WORKFLOW?

Keep business contact research organized

Research often starts with several locations and many search result pages. A repeatable workflow makes it easier to collect and review findings without maintaining scattered notes.

01

Start with locations

Import a CSV or Excel spreadsheet containing a City column, or add locations manually.

02

Review visible addresses

Collect email addresses present in the text of Google search result pages. Linked websites are not crawled.

03

Reduce duplicate entries

Keep a deduplicated list so repeated appearances of an address do not clutter your results.

i

Important limitations and responsible use

CityMail Finder does not verify whether an address is active, does not crawl linked websites and does not send email. Google may show CAPTCHA challenges or limit automated requests. Complete any verification manually and respect provider terms, privacy rules and applicable marketing laws. Do not use results for unsolicited bulk email.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Business email finder FAQ

Does CityMail Finder find every email for a business?

No. It only extracts addresses visible in Google search result text for the searches you run. It cannot guarantee complete or current contact information.

Does it visit company websites?

No. The current workflow does not crawl linked business websites or inspect their contact pages.

Can I export the results?

Yes. You can export the unique collected email addresses to an Excel workbook.
