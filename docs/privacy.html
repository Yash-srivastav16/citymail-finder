CityMail Finder Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy describes the current browser extension behaviour in the CityMail Finder repository. It should be reviewed and updated if the product adds accounts, analytics, cloud storage, payments or additional search providers.

Information you provide

You may enter city names, configure search queries and import a CSV or Excel file containing a City column. The extension processes this information to perform the search workflow you initiate.

Search activity

When you start a search, the extension opens Google Search pages in your browser. Your browser sends search requests to Google. Google may process those requests under its own privacy policy and terms.

Collected results and local storage

Email addresses visible in Google search result page text are collected by the extension. The current extension stores result records in your browser's IndexedDB and saves city settings, page settings and search progress in browser local storage. The current extension workflow does not upload the collected email list to a CityMail Finder account or server.

CAPTCHA and verification

If Google displays a verification challenge, the workflow pauses and you must complete the challenge yourself in the browser. CityMail Finder does not solve or bypass CAPTCHA challenges.

Permissions

The extension requests browser storage permissions to retain settings and results, tabs permission to open and manage search tabs, and scripting permission to read visible text on the Google Search pages used by the workflow. Review the extension manifest in the public repository for the current permission list.

Data sharing and sale

CityMail Finder does not sell the result list or transmit it to a CityMail Finder account in the current extension workflow. Search requests are made to Google from your browser. Do not collect, export or use personal data in ways that violate applicable privacy, data protection, anti-spam or marketing laws.

Retention and deletion

Results remain in the browser's local extension storage until you clear the extension's stored data or remove the extension and its data through your browser. You are responsible for handling exported spreadsheets securely.

Children

CityMail Finder is a business research tool and is not designed for children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the extension's functionality or data handling changes. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact and support

For questions or to report an issue, use the CityMail Finder GitHub Issues page.
